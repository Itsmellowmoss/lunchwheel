# Lunch Wheel

## Start the website on macOS

Double-click `start.command`. It starts a local web server and opens the Lunch Wheel in your browser. Keep the Terminal window open while using it; press Ctrl+C in that window to stop the server.

If double-clicking is blocked, open Terminal, run `cd` followed by a space, drag this folder into Terminal, press Return, then run `python3 serve.py`.

The page starts with its saved places and checks OpenStreetMap within about a 20-minute walking range, split into four smaller map areas, for restaurants, cafes, fast food, food courts, bakeries, delis, sandwich shops, and any published opening hours. Results from successful areas are kept if another area fails. Places with known opening hours must overlap the 11:00–14:00 lunch window on at least one day to appear; places with unknown or incomplete hours are kept. Search the shortlist by place name, address, cuisine, or type. Filter by one or more place types using the checkbox menu, opening status, price confirmation, vegetarian options, walking time (in 5-minute steps), or price. With no place types checked, all types are shown. **Include closed places on the wheel** and **Show and spin places without confirmed prices** are enabled by default. Unpriced places can be hidden from both the shortlist and wheel by unchecking the price-confirmation option. Open places sort first, followed by hours-unconfirmed places and then closed places. Each place has a green open, red closed, or yellow hours-unconfirmed dot. List entries stay compact until hovered or focused to reveal their details. When closed places are excluded from the wheel, they remain visible at the bottom of the list in grey. Hours-unconfirmed places remain eligible for the wheel but are never labeled as confirmed open. Removed places can be restored with **Undo** or from the empty-shortlist prompt. If the nearby lookup is unavailable, the saved list remains usable. Google Maps does not allow this page to fetch private business details directly.

Each place has an **Edit** button for updating its name, type, address, approximate walking time, price range, vegetarian information, and weekly hours. Changes are saved in this browser’s local storage and persist across reloads on the same browser and device. Only the fields you change are saved, so later updates to the saved list still appear for edited places. Clearing browser site data removes those edits.

The saved list and its Google Maps hours are stored in `restaurants.js`, with each weekly schedule written directly in that restaurant's `hours` object. The list includes distinct Google Maps listings found around Sveavägen 47 within an approximately 1.4 km straight-line radius, including restaurants, fast food, cafes, and lunch-oriented bakeries. This is a manually collected shortlist, not an exhaustive directory: Google Maps search is relevance-ranked and may omit nearby businesses. Places with known opening hours must overlap 11:00–14:00 on at least one day; two dinner-only places were removed, and the same rule prevents nearby map results from adding them back. Places with unknown or incomplete schedules remain visible. Weekly hours shown on Google Maps were copied for the remaining saved places on 9 November 2026; these are listing hours, not independently confirmed business statements. The Los Tacos Sveavägen 88 listing was matched and its hours added on that date. Prices are recorded only when a current official menu or ordering page provides prices for the matching branch; the cited items and source links appear in each expanded entry. Other prices remain unconfirmed when an official branch price could not be verified; as of 9 November 2026, 32 of the 70 saved places have a sourced price and 38 remain unconfirmed. Prices from another branch, another city, a chain's national menu, or a search snippet are never used. Prices and availability can change, so check the linked menu before relying on them. Vegetarian options were checked against official menus or, where none was available, the branch's own delivery-app listing; search-result pages and review sites are not accepted as evidence. As of 9 November 2026, 48 of the 70 saved places have a documented vegetarian meal option, while 22 remain unconfirmed. Unconfirmed means the research did not verify an option, not that the restaurant has none; each unconfirmed place's note says why. No place was marked as having no vegetarian options. Some places show a highlighted note, for example when they serve no weekday lunch or have announced a move. Expand a place to see its note and source link when available. Walking times are estimates.

The wheel uses the Europe/Stockholm timezone. Click **Refresh nearby data** to retry the live lookup; cached map data is reused for up to two hours. Walking times for places found on the map are estimates, and the linked directions show the route.

Lunch-spot rows stay compact until hovered or focused, then expand to reveal their details and controls. A green dot means open, red means closed, and yellow means hours are unconfirmed. Hover over a wheel slice to expand it and reveal the place’s type, hours status, address, walking estimate, and price details.
